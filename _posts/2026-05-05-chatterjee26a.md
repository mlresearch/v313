title: A Martingale Kernel Two-Sample Test
abstract: The Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD) is a widely used multivariate distance
  metric for two-sample testing. The standard MMD test statistic has an intractable
  null distribution typically requiring costly resampling or permutation approaches
  for calibration. In this work we leverage a martingale interpretation of the estimated
  squared MMD to propose martingale MMD (mMMD), a quadratic-time statistic which has
  a limiting standard Gaussian distribution under the null. Moreover we show that
  the test is consistent against any fixed alternative and for large sample sizes,
  mMMD offers substantial computational savings over the standard MMD test, with only
  a minor loss in power.
