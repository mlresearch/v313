title: No Scale Sensitive Dimension for Distribution Learning
abstract: Learning probability distributions is one of the most basic statistical
  learning tasks. While for many learning tasks learnability of a class can be characterized
  by a combinatorial dimension (like the VC-dimension for binary classification prediction),
  no such characterization is known for classes of probability distributions. A leap
  toward resolving this long-standing problem was made recently by Lechner and Ben-David
  who showed that there can be no \emph{scale invariant} characterization of PAC style
  learnability of such classes. The question of \emph{scale sensitive} characterization
  remained open. In this paper we fully resolve the question by showing that there
  can be no \emph{scale sensitive} combinatorial characterization of PAC style learnability
  of classes of probability distributions.
